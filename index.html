About Our Church

Welcome to Latter Rain Mission (Zimbabwe), a vibrant community of believers dedicated to spiritual growth, worship, and service. Our mission is to nurture disciples of Christ through biblical teaching, fellowship, and outreach programs.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of hope and love in our communities, empowering individuals to live out their faith boldly and compassionately.

Who We Are

We are a faith-based community rooted in biblical teachings, striving to create a welcoming space for people of all ages to worship, learn, and grow together in Christ.

Our Beliefs

We believe in the transformative power of the Gospel, the importance of community, and the call to serve others with love and humility.

What We Do

Youth Programs & Empowerment

We believe in nurturing the next generation of leaders. Through dynamic youth programs, we encourage young people to gather, share insights, and grow in faith. From Bible studies to mentorship and creative arts, we provide a supportive environment for spiritual and social growth.

Multi-Congregational Fellowship

Our church family extends across Zimbabwe, with multiple congregations in different regions. We hold joint services, conferences, and outreach events to strengthen our unity in Christ.

Love in Action

We demonstrate God’s love through community outreach—feeding the hungry, supporting vulnerable families, and visiting the sick—to make a tangible difference in people’s lives.

Worship & Discipleship

At our core is authentic worship and discipleship. Through uplifting services and small-group fellowships, we help believers deepen their relationship with God.

Our Mission

To inspire hope, share the Gospel, and build a Christ-centered community where everyone feels valued and empowered to fulfill God’s purpose.

Our Values

  • Faith: Trusting in God’s promises and guidance.
  • Community: Building meaningful relationships with one another.
  • Service: Serving others with love and humility.
  • Integrity: Upholding biblical principles in all we do.

Join us as we journey together in faith, growing in love and service to our communities and the world.
